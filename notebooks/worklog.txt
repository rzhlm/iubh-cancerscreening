## 1. Business Understanding

 A group of oncologists want to become more familiar with data science models
 They especially want to have explainability, and understanding the most important features for the predictions.

Task:
Develop multiple classification models, predict: tumor malignant or benign, with high accuracy (F1 score > 0.95). 
The models should be interpretable. 
Investigate feature importance on different models.
The accuracy should be high, but the interpretability of the model is the final aim.
The outcomes should be presented/communicated to non-experts to convince them about the trustworthiness.


To help develop confidence in the models, discuss the importance of each explanatory variable
and make the model results as interpretable as possible. 
In addition, a detailed error analysis is very important, to understand the weaknesses of the approach. 

Propose how to integrate into daily work of the doctors, e.g. GUI schematic

## 2. Data understanding

Assess the quality of the data set provided.
